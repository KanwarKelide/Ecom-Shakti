Prepare now
RISKS & HOW TO BEAT THEM
Risks to watch
⚠️
Unrealistic client expectations
Sellers expect guaranteed sales results. Platforms change; outcomes aren't guaranteed — effort and optimization are.
⚠️
Churn without visible results in 30 days
Clients who don't see improvement within a month will leave. Speed of initial results is critical.
⚠️
Price undercutting by freelancers
Fiverr/Upwork sellers offer the same service for ₹500. Win on trust, speed, and local-language support — not price.
⚠️
Client concentration risk
If top 3 clients = 60%+ of MRR, you're exposed. Always be acquiring new clients in parallel.
How to beat each risk
✓
Signed service agreement
Clear list of what's included, what's not, and what outcomes are realistic. Protects you legally and sets expectations right.
✓
Weekly WhatsApp progress updates
Send updates every Friday — screenshots of changes made. Perceived value = visible activity.
✓
Build trust, not price
Hindi support, local understanding, fast response time — these are moats a ₹500 freelancer can't replicate.
✓
SOPs from day 1
Document every process in writing. Quality stays consistent as you scale and hire. Prevents single points of failure.
