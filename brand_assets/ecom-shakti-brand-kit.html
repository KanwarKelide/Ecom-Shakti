04
Brand Voice & Copy
How Ecom Shakti speaks — bold, desi, direct
💪
Bold
Confident and direct. We know what we do and say it plainly. No hedging.
🇮🇳
Desi
Hindi-English mix. "Apni sales 2X karein" — we speak like our clients.
⚡
Fast
Quick replies, quick fixes. No corporate delay or jargon.
📊
Proof-led
Numbers and results first. Before/after screenshots beat any claim.
Write like this ✓
"Listing reject ho gayi? Hum 24 ghante mein fix karte hain."
Not like this ✗
"Our comprehensive platform optimization solutions leverage synergistic methodologies to enhance your marketplace visibility."
Write like this ✓
"1000+ sellers supported. Account suspended kiya? WhatsApp karo — free audit milega."
Not like this ✗
"We are pleased to offer our e-commerce account management services to help facilitate your online retail journey."
CTA examples ✓
"WhatsApp Karo Abhi →"
"Free Audit Lo →"
"Sales 2X Karein →"
"Hum Fix Karenge →"
CTAs to avoid ✗
"Submit your inquiry"
"Learn more about our services"
"Contact us today"
"Request a consultation"
